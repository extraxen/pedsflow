PedsFlow v18.4.0 — Native Pediatric DKA Integration
Implemented: July 31, 2026

Changes:
- Replaced the externally embedded DKA website with a native Flutter module.
- Added CPS/TREKK-aligned DKA assessment, severity, corrected sodium, anion gap,
  effective osmolality, HHS screening, fluid rates, two-bag calculations,
  insulin and potassium safety logic, cerebral-injury treatment calculations,
  monitoring trends, alerts, and a copyable handoff summary.
- Added native DKA engine unit tests.
- Removed iframe and url_launcher dependency.
- Preserved PedsFlow custom icon, complete PCCU content, and existing calculators.

Clinical limitation:
This module is decision support only. Verify calculations and treatment against
current local DKA pathways and involve endocrinology/PCCU/pharmacy as indicated.
