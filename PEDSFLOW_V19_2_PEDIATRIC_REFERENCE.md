# PedsFlow v19.2 — CPS Bilirubin & Pediatric Reference

## Added
- Advanced CPS-aligned neonatal bilirubin assessment for infants ≥35 weeks
- Embedded gestational-age-specific phototherapy and exchange thresholds
- ΔTSB, TcB confirmation, rate-of-rise, neurotoxicity risk and pre-exchange logic
- Interactive threshold graph and CPS treatment/rebound reference
- Pediatric vital-sign age bands, MAP calculator and PALS hypotension thresholds
- WHO growth assessment from birth through 18 years with z-scores and percentiles

## Safety
This release is clinical decision support. Threshold data and boundary behavior must be independently reviewed against the current CPS statement and local neonatal policy before production clinical use.
