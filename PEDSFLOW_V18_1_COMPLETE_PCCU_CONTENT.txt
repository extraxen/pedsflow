PedsFlow v18.1.0 — Complete PCCU Clinical Content Rebuild
Implemented: July 26, 2026

This release replaces the duplicated PCCU placeholder text with topic-specific clinical content across all 14 PCCU categories and all listed topics.

Each page now contains:
- Clinical overview
- Initial stabilization
- Disease-specific treatment or settings
- Monitoring
- Expected response
- Failure criteria and escalation
- Complications and cautions
- Sources and local verification
- Copyable PCCU plan

High-priority respiratory, airway, asthma, ARDS, septic shock, status epilepticus, raised ICP, DKA, cerebral injury, hyperkalemia and anaphylaxis pathways were rewritten with detailed bespoke content. All remaining pages use category- and topic-specific clinical guidance rather than the previous identical scaffold.

This remains clinician decision support only. Verify current local protocols, doses, concentrations and equipment standards before implementation.
