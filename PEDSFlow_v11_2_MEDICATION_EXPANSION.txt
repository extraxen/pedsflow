PedsFlow v11.2.0 — Medication Expansion

Medication database expanded from 300 to 386 monographs.
Added 86 high-yield medications after a systematic gap audit.

Major additions:
- Toxicology antidotes and rescue therapies
- Anticoagulants, reversal agents, blood products and hematology drugs
- Constipation, IBD, liver and nutrition medications
- Electrolyte, renal and fluid therapies
- Neonatal surfactants, caffeine, nitric oxide and RSV prophylaxis
- Tumor lysis and metabolic-emergency agents
- Resistant-organism antimicrobials and tropical medicine drugs

All new monographs are intentionally marked for local verification. High-alert therapy must be checked against the current hospital formulary, pharmacy, Ontario Poison Centre, and relevant specialty protocol.
