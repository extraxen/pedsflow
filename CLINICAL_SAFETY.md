# Clinical Safety

PedsFlow is an educational and clinical-support tool. It does not replace independent medical judgment, current institutional order sets, pharmacist consultation, product monographs, or supervising physician decisions.

The uploaded PCCU pocket card is dated May 23, 2003 and is shown only in a clearly separated historical-local tab. The app prioritizes current Canadian sources when imported.

Do not store patient identifiers or protected health information in notes or images.
