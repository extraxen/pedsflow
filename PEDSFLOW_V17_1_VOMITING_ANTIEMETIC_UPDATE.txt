PedsFlow v17.1.0 — Vomiting & Antiemetic Update
Implemented July 25, 2026

Medication updates:
- Expanded ondansetron, dimenhydrinate, diphenhydramine, metoclopramide, domperidone, promethazine, lorazepam and olanzapine safety/dosing sections.
- Added prochlorperazine, droperidol, haloperidol, topical capsaicin and aprepitant monographs.
- Added QT, electrolyte, sedation and extrapyramidal-reaction safeguards.

Admission-plan updates:
- Acute gastroenteritis with dehydration
- Migraine and status migrainosus
- Undifferentiated abdominal pain
- Bowel obstruction/bilious vomiting
- Acute pancreatitis
- Feeding intolerance/vomiting in medically complex children
- Pediatric cannabis ingestion
- New Cannabis Hyperemesis Syndrome (CHS) plan

All treatment guidance requires confirmation with the current local formulary, pharmacist, institutional pathway and attending physician.
