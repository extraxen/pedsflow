PedsFlow v11.0 update

Implemented:
- Admission Plans search now searches diagnosis titles, aliases, and specialty/category only.
- Home/global search now returns admission plans only; medications and antibiotics remain searchable in their own tabs.
- Added detailed plans for:
  1. Acetaminophen (Tylenol) overdose
  2. Neonatal HSV infection / HSV encephalitis
  3. Hypokalemia
  4. Hypophosphatemia
  5. Refeeding syndrome
- Updated app version to 11.0.0+1100.

Clinical safety:
- Acetaminophen management points to the Ontario Poison Centre protocol effective April 1, 2026.
- Neonatal HSV content includes the second-week presentation and unexplained transaminitis as important warning features.
- High-alert IV potassium/phosphate preparation and infusion limits remain linked to current local pharmacy/institutional protocols.

Open in VS Code:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the folder containing pubspec.yaml.
3. Run: flutter pub get
4. Run: flutter analyze
5. Run: flutter run -d chrome

PedsFlow v11.1.0 — Toxicology expansion
Added 12 practical pediatric toxicology modules: cannabis, beta-blocker, calcium-channel blocker, clonidine/alpha-2 agonist, sulfonylurea, anticholinergic/antihistamine, hydrocarbon, lithium, methemoglobinemia, isoniazid, digoxin, and toxic seizure/severe agitation. All high-risk antidote and infusion pathways explicitly require Ontario Poison Centre and local PICU/pharmacy confirmation.
