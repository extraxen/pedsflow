PedsFlow targeted clinical update
Version: 20.4.0+2040
Release date: August 23, 2026

Simplified potassium-rate checker:
- Replaces the complex stackable hypokalemia builder with a four-step bedside workflow.
- Calculates exact potassium delivery from potassium-containing maintenance/base IV fluids.
- Adds an optional TPN/second IV potassium source.
- Supports intermittent KCl and potassium phosphate.
- Potassium phosphate can be entered by potassium content or phosphate content.
- Shows total IV potassium in mmol/hr and mmol/kg/hr.
- Shows whether each background IV source continues or is paused during replacement.
- Provides scenario comparison for continuing all IV potassium vs pausing the main potassium-containing fluid.
- Separates the KCl ECG-monitoring threshold from the KCl intermittent maximum and the all-IV potassium maximum.
- Applies the pediatric LHSC potassium-phosphate maximum of 0.19 mmol potassium/kg/hr.
- Checks intermittent peripheral/central potassium concentrations.
- Oral/enteral potassium is explicitly excluded from the IV rate ceiling.
- Adds unit tests for exact-rate arithmetic and patient-specific policy thresholds.

Clinical source basis:
- LHSC Potassium Chloride Parenteral Drug Administration Monograph, revised July 21, 2026.
- LHSC Potassium Phosphate Parenteral Drug Administration Monograph, revised July 21, 2026.

Important:
- This calculator is arithmetic/decision support, not an automatic prescribing system.
- Local order sets, pharmacy guidance, clinical monitoring, renal function and patient-specific factors remain authoritative.
