# PedsFlow 10.0 Release Notes

## Admission library

- Expanded from 130 to exactly **200 pediatric admission plans**.
- Added 70 structured plans across:
  - respiratory medicine
  - infectious diseases
  - endocrine and metabolic emergencies
  - hematology and oncology
  - neurology
  - gastroenterology
  - nephrology and urology
  - cardiology
  - toxicology
  - surgery and orthopedics
  - psychiatry, child protection and complex care

## Search and navigation

- Added diagnosis aliases, including:
  - CVST
  - CLABSI
  - SVC syndrome
  - TCA overdose
  - toxic alcohol
  - PTA
  - OSA
- Search now includes aliases, guidance text and source labels.
- Admission-plan counts are dynamic rather than hard-coded.
- Added a Clinical Sources page.

## Content governance

Every admission plan now supports:

- aliases
- content status
- last-updated date
- source hierarchy

The 70 new plans are original structured clinical-support summaries.
They are explicitly labelled for local clinical review and do not replace
hospital order sets or consultant-specific pathways.

## Unchanged

- 300-medication catalogue
- Medication favourites, history and quality dashboard
- Antibiotics & Organisms module
- 20 calculators
- Backup and restore
- Personal notes, plan edits, favourites and algorithm library
