PedsFlow v16.0.0 — Integrated ED/PICU Clinical Decision Support

Added one integrated module containing:
- v13 electrolyte replacement decision-support calculators for potassium, phosphate, magnesium, calcium and sodium.
- v14 EMR-copyable admission order sets for general pediatrics, sepsis, DKA, status epilepticus, respiratory distress, dehydration/electrolytes, toxic ingestion and PICU.
- v15 syndrome-based antibiotic decision support with spectrum symbols, allergy logic, renal/hepatic prompts, culture/source-control prompts and stewardship checks.
- v16 pediatric emergency/PICU algorithms for stabilization, ventilation, vasoactives, status epilepticus, DKA, sepsis, toxicology and PALS structure.

Safety design:
- Required patient context before electrolyte recommendations.
- Hard-stop warnings for ECG changes, arrhythmia, severe renal failure, altered mental status and high-risk physiology.
- No unsupported universal high-risk doses or infusion maximums.
- Every module carries the verification statement:
  “Verify with local institutional protocols, formulary, pharmacist, and attending physician before implementation.”

Navigation:
- New Clinical tab in the bottom navigation.
- New ED/PICU decision support entry on the home screen.
