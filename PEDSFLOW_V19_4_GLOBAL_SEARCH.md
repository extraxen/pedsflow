# PedsFlow v19.4.0 — Full-App Global Search

## What is indexed
- Full admission-plan content: titles, aliases, summaries, all sections, treatments, guidance, sources, and local edited section/treatment text.
- Full medication monographs: generic names, aliases/brand names, summaries, dose sections, routes, administration, monitoring, warnings, sources, linked plans.
- All PCCU topic pages and their embedded clinical content.
- All calculator titles/subtitles plus DKA and CPS bilirubin feature content.
- Pain-management topics and their clinical content.
- Antibiotic syndromes, organisms, treatment approach, linked drugs, and coverage pearls.
- ED/PICU decision-support launch tools.
- Escalation guidance, pediatric references, medication-reference pages, clinical-source pages, and saved algorithm titles/categories/notes.

## Search behavior
- One unified ranked result list.
- Exact title and alias matches rank highest.
- Phrase/title-prefix/content ranking.
- Common pediatric abbreviations and brand names.
- Typo tolerance using bounded Levenshtein matching.
- Query debounce for smooth typing.
- Category filters.
- Direct navigation to admission plans, medication monographs, and exact PCCU topics.
